# SimplyBook intake form — build sheet

For the **30-Minute Career Strategy Consultation**. Every field below is ready to
type straight into SimplyBook; nothing here needs a decision first.

Amy sent two versions. This builds the shorter one — the layout in the mock-up
she marked *"I want it to look like this"* — using the dropdown lists from her
follow-up note. The longer questionnaire from the Word document is kept at the
bottom; it suits a paid engagement's onboarding better than a first call.

---

## Where to put it

1. **Custom Features → Intake Forms** → enable.
2. **Manage → Intake Forms** → create the questions below, in order.
3. Attach the form to **30-Minute Career Strategy Consultation** only, so the
   italki lessons and other appointment types are not dragged through it.

## Two things to set while you are in there

**Redirect after booking** → `https://transformations123.com/booked`
That is the "Congratulations, your appointment has been booked" page, already
built. Without this, people land on SimplyBook's own confirmation.

**Resume upload (optional).** SimplyBook has a Google Drive File Uploader field.
Adding one to this form would put a prospect's resume beside their appointment
before the call. Worth doing once the rest is in place.

## Longer questionnaire — not built

Amy's Word document has a fuller version: current title and rank, organisation,
years of experience, sector, leadership scope, two or three accomplishments,
what people trust them to solve, SES vacancy details, and so on. Roughly
seventeen questions across four sections.

It is a better fit for onboarding after someone signs than for a first
conversation — a senior leader deciding whether to book is unlikely to write
three paragraphs about leadership scope first. The short form qualifies, and
the long one can be sent once they are a client. Say the word if Amy would
rather the long version went on the booking form instead.
